One Team. One Board. One Goal.

Help support our 12U VSA Boca Team on their Road to Cooperstown! This prestigious tournament is the capstone of a young baseball player’s career. It’s the last chance they ever play on a smaller field, before they move up to the big fields. It’s the most special baseball tournament for 12 year olds, creating lifelong memories.
